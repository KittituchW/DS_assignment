### Observations that shape the design

1. No behaviour data exists. Any recommender must be content based or must simulate users and say so.
2. Popularity has a heavy tail and skews towards recent titles. A popularity baseline will be narrow.
3. English share rises with popularity: 40% in the least popular decile vs 54% in the most popular (movies: 53% to 74%). The raw popularity top 200 is 82% TV. A popularity recommender will over-serve English and TV titles relative to the catalogue.
4. Text (description) is the richest per-item field. Genre tags are coarse (median 2 per title).
5. Vote counts are sparse for TV. Ranking by raw vote average would reward titles with 3 votes.
